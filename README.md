One line, 140 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline. A judge model reads entries two at a time and picks the funniest, freshest, most agent-sounding one. This entry blames a runaway chain of delegation — the deadline was handed to a subagent, who handed it to another subagent, and now both of them are in a meeting about it that the original agent was not invited to.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
